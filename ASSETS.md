# Visual assets

`public/images/hero.jpg` is an original image generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then JPEG-optimized locally. The unbranded fictional bottle is brand mood imagery, not a catalog product photograph. The original generation output remains in the Codex generated-images folder.

Prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: luxury fragrance website hero photograph, landscape 1536x1024. Photorealistic cinematic editorial still life: a single unbranded rectangular amber glass perfume bottle with a sculptural dark brown cap and blank cream label, standing on rough ivory travertine, dried branch and small bergamot peel nearby. Warm late afternoon sun from upper right, deep espresso shadows, background warm taupe plaster wall. Bottle in right third, left half softly shadowed empty wall for white headline overlay. Sophisticated niche perfumery campaign, tactile analog film grain, restrained, expensive, art directed. No text, logos, watermark. This is original brand mood imagery, not a real product photo.

The monogram/favicon are original SVG artwork. The initial fictitious CSS bottle studies have been removed from the interface.

33 reviewed product photographs are stored in `public/images/bottles/` under CC BY-SA 3.0 from Open Beauty Facts, with per-record contributor credit, source and license links in `src/data/fragrances.json`. Every other catalog record has an externally hosted product-image reference from a public product/source page or image search. Those 1,292 images are not downloaded or locally redistributed; each detail view links to its source, and the project does not claim a license for those works. External image availability and reuse terms can change. `data/sources/photo-review.json` records the approved mappings for the Open Beauty Facts photos. See `DATA-LICENSES.md`. Lucide icons are provided by the installed `lucide-react` package under its ISC license. Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans are Google Fonts offered under the SIL Open Font License; runtime fallbacks are provided.

## Catalog bottle-study illustrations

`public/images/fragrance-illustrations/` contains one original, locally generated SVG bottle study for every catalog record. They are shown as the primary image where a suitable product photo was unavailable and are used as automatic local fallbacks if a remote product image fails to load. Each illustration uses the same minimalist clear-glass bottle, dark cap, warm ivory label and neutral studio background, with brand and fragrance printed on the label. These are stylistic illustrations, not product photographs or depictions of verified packaging. The product details view labels them accordingly. They are generated by `scripts/generate-template-bottle-art.py`.
